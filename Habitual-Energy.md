Habit is an acquired collectivity of personal history, which reveals an individual's unconsciousness. This project shows this addictive energy that only 'habit' has, which tell habits from general acts. It is defined as 'habitual energy'. The team made a hypothetical character who researches habitual energy and set the place as his laboratory. For having strong storytelling which includes performance, and an unique character regarding the theme, Habitual Energy, this project won the grand prize.
