# Life Helmet

![](/images/Life-Helmet/helmet_side_lit.jpg)

Life Helmet is concept art for a wearable refuge. It gives instant shelter in the moments when what is socially required of you and what you actually feel come into conflict.

When the wearer pulls a trigger, CO2 fills the tubes and the helmet inflates around the head, the way a life raft opens. Inside it, the wearer can leave a frustrating situation completely, both visually and audibly. In the concept, the helmet also holds a speaker and a display that respond to the wearer's brain waves and play back whatever the moment calls for.

*Sellotape, microcontroller, optical fiber, 0.3m x 0.3m x 0.3m*
